# Pakistan public-source audit

Reviewed 19 September 2026. This is a partial reference snapshot, not a complete or live order of battle. Wikipedia articles were individually reviewed against their infoboxes and organization tables; dated primary reports corroborate the service chiefs, fleet commander and Southern Air Command commander. Secondary tables do not establish present operational disposition.

## Coverage

| Service | Records | Included detail |
|---|---:|---|
| army | 38 | Nine field corps, 24 divisions, Army Aviation, two specialist commands and Pakistan Military Academy. |
| navy | 45 | Nine commands, two fleet components, 21 ships/submarines and 12 shore establishments. |
| airforce | 34 | Seven commands, 12 air bases/academies and 14 flying squadrons. |

Total: 117 organizations and 101 registered references. Counts indicate included records, not completeness.

## Evidence and exclusions

- Army corps headquarters and divisional associations are drawn from individual corps articles and the Army structure reference. Divisions remain tree-only; no headquarters or deployment coordinate is inferred. Corps leader names are undated reference observations, not verified appointment dates.
- Navy vessels are unlocated assets under their documented fleet/type association. The Hangor entry uses a dated June 2026 public arrival report as evidence of service; the map never displays its reported or live position. Auxiliary and patrol fleets remain incomplete.
- PAF squadrons are children of their publicly associated base. The separately sourced command field is optional. No. 27 Squadron has conflicting Northern/Central command references: the command is withheld, while the Rafiqui association is retained. Nur Khan, Samungli and the academy are service-level associations where a current command parent is not established.
- Aircraft subvariants are omitted where references conflict. The dataset is not a current aircraft inventory. New cyber/space structures were not added without sufficient independent evidence.
- All mapped coordinates are city/locality centroids. No military jurisdiction polygons, weapon locations or live deployments are derived.
