# Ranks & insignia — coverage and research notes

Reference snapshot checked 20 September 2026. This is a within-country educational comparison, not a cross-country equivalence or NATO grade chart.

## Coverage

India, Pakistan and China: Army, Navy and Air Force officer and other-rank ladders; exceptional five-star and training ranks separated where relevant. India NCC: Army, Naval and Air wings, with cadets, Senior Division/Wing Associate NCC Officers and Junior Division/Wing ANOs in separate categories.

The version-controlled records are in `data/ranks/rows.json`. Each row cites references in `sources.json`; artwork and its author/license are in `assets.json`. Selecting a rank exposes those references and image credits. Original Wikimedia artwork is hosted locally, unchanged. Generic naval sleeve and air-force braid illustrations are used only where the cited service rank table identifies that design; filenames referring to another service or NATO code do not establish equivalence.

## India

DG NCC's Army JD/JW handbook, printed pages 20–21, provides an explicit tri-service table for officers and other ranks. This is the basis for all regular Indian cross-service rows, including Lance Naik / Seaman I / Leading Aircraftsman. Do not substitute pension-group equivalence tables, which can group multiple grades for a different purpose.

- [Official NCC handbook](https://ncc.kerala.gov.in/images/pdf/handbook/army_jr/ARMY_JD_JW_WEB_FINAL.pdf#page=29)
- [Army insignia reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Army_ranks_and_insignia)
- [Navy insignia reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Navy_ranks_and_insignia)
- [Air Force insignia reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Air_Force_ranks_and_insignia)

The naval admiral shoulder boards use the newer Indian design documented in the current reference table. Field Marshal and Marshal of the Indian Air Force are separate from the normal promotion ladder. Admiral of the Fleet is listed in the NCC handbook but has never been conferred; no insignia is fabricated. Midshipman remains in the training category.

## Pakistan

Current service tables provide officer and JCO structures, checked against the US Marine Corps country handbook. The latter is older and is not treated as evidence of current leadership or current uniform design. Current PAF imagery uses the post-2006 design in the service reference, not historical RAF-style images.

- [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Army_ranks_and_insignia)
- [Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Navy_ranks_and_insignia)
- [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Air_Force_ranks_and_insignia)
- [USMC country handbook](https://info.publicintelligence.net/MCIA-PakistanHandbook.pdf)

Senior Technician and Corporal Technician counterparts are supported by their published rank references. No exact match is asserted for Chief Technician, the remaining lower PAF grades or the lower Army/Naval grades where reviewed references disagree. These appear on their own rows with empty counterpart cells. Midshipman is not forced into the Second Lieutenant / Pilot Officer row. Marshal of the Pakistan Air Force is shown as a listed exceptional rank with no verified holder.

## China

The shared Chinese grades determine the comparison. The English title Senior Captain is used for the naval 大校 grade. Chinese labels are retained because translations vary. A Chinese General is not compared to another country's General.

The seven NCO grades and two conscript grades use the 2022 system. 一级上士 and 二级上士 replace outdated middle-grade labels. Original insignia filenames can retain older grade abbreviations without representing the displayed rank names.

- [Official 2022 reform explanation](https://www.mva.gov.cn/sy/xx/gfxx/202204/t20220426_59960.html)
- [Ground Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranks_of_the_People%27s_Liberation_Army_Ground_Force)
- [Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranks_of_the_People%27s_Liberation_Army_Navy)
- [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranks_of_the_People%27s_Liberation_Army_Air_Force)

## NCC: explicit gaps

[NIT Puducherry's institutional table](https://www.nitpy.ac.in/students/studentActivities/NCC) supports the Army–Air cadet mapping and the senior two naval comparisons. Naval middle-grade names and alignment conflict with [the general NCC reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cadet_Corps_(India)). These naval grades are listed independently rather than treating a colspan in a Wikipedia table as evidence of equivalence. Company Sergeant Major is an Army-wing appointment without a confirmed direct counterpart here. Institutional alternate names are noted in details.

ANO names follow Schedule III of the [NCC Act and Rules](https://www.ncc.du.ac.in/downloads/NCC%20ACT%20and%20Rules%20%20Dec%201948.pdf), with current Army entry/category names cross-checked against the [2024 Kerala Government order](https://document.kerala.gov.in/documents/governmentorders/govtorder3011202412%3A52%3A44.pdf). Legacy Second Lieutenant and Pilot Officer entry names in the old Rules are not asserted as current ANO grades. The common JD/JW ladder is Third, Second, First and Chief Officer.

**NCC-specific ANO insignia artwork has not been verified.** Those cells show the rank name and an explicit artwork gap. Regular-service badges are not relabelled as NCC badges. This gap is not a claim that ANOs wear no insignia. Cadet ranks with no rank insignia use a separate label.

## Updates

To correct a name or equivalence, change the row and attach an explicit reference. Leave unknown counterparts null. Add authentic artwork only with an identified rank, source page, creator, license and checked date; do not infer a badge by rank name. Tests validate record integrity and UI behavior, not historical truth. Research gaps above require human source review.
