# Karnataka & Goa NCC organizational register

This register contains six documented groups and 55 unit records, reviewed on 12 September 2026. It combines a systematic government baseline with subsequent institutional publications. It is not a certified current establishment return: some unit identities are recently corroborated while their group relationship or headquarters city remains supported only by older evidence. Two records are explicitly historical-only in the explorer.

The directorate covers Karnataka and Goa together. Group boundaries have not been established by the cited material, so the map does not partition the directorate using group headquarters or college locations. College contingents, companies within colleges and training-camp participants are not counted as independent NCC battalions.

## 4 Karnataka Engineer Company NCC

4 Karnataka Engineer Company is an Army Wing NCC company with a publicly documented office at MIT Campus, Manipal. The explorer places it at a conservative Manipal city point. SMVITM’s unit profile identifies the office; its separate RDC 2025 report explicitly links the company to Mangaluru Group and the Karnataka & Goa Directorate. The same parent and city appear in the 2017 parliamentary register. [SMVITM profile](https://sode-edu.in/smvitm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/NCC-Content.pdf), [RDC 2025 report](https://sode-edu.in/smvitm/accomplishments/cadet-chm-arpita-thantry-to-represent-4-kar-engr-coy-ncc-as-main-dancer-at-republic-day-camp-2025/).

Lt Col P. S. Chauhan is identified as Officer Commanding by SMVITM. A June 2025 camp-closing report corroborates that role, and YIT reports Lt Col P. S. Chouhan leading the unit’s rank-distribution ceremony on 13 August 2025. The explorer retains the initials and notes the spelling difference. An appointment date and September 2026 incumbency are not established. No officer portrait with both reliable identification and established reuse rights was found. [Camp report](https://www.daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1282568), [YIT ceremony](https://yit.edu.in/activities.php).

The institutional associations are deliberately separate from the unit hierarchy. MIT is identified as the public office campus; SMVITM documents affiliation since 2020 and cadet B/C certificate activity; YIT documents its cadets’ participation in the unit ceremony. A complete sanctioned list of affiliated institutions was not located. SMVITM’s institutional intake must not be presented as the entire engineer company’s strength. Associate NCC Officers and caretaker officers at colleges are separate from the company’s Officer Commanding. [SMVITM NCC](https://sode-edu.in/smvitm/ncc/).

Older reports identify different company leaders. Those reports do not establish present incumbency or justify combining photographs with the latest name. A third-party upload of a directorate magazine supplies additional leads, including an expanded officer name, but its publication authority, date and several roster details remain unresolved. Those claims are not promoted to verified facts.

## Scope and evidence

The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports’ Lok Sabha answer Q1791 of 28 December 2017 lists Karnataka & Goa’s directorate, six group headquarters and 54 subordinate units in Annexure A, printed PDF pages 6–8. The current register includes those historical unit identities plus the already documented 5 Karnataka Battalion. This baseline is a checklist for corroboration, not a claim that all 54 remain unchanged. [Parliamentary register](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/13/AU1791.pdf?source=pqals).

A source’s access date is distinct from its publication date, the date of an event, and the date an appointment began. An undated college page supports the text observed on that page; it does not create a 2026 appointment or prove uninterrupted subordination. Institutional reports about camps support unit identity and sometimes city or parent. Merely attending another group’s camp does not establish a parent relationship.

The six-group structure is corroborated by the BMSCE institutional NCC page. The Goa government identified Air Commodore Kamal Chadha as DDG and Col C. Rajashekar as Belagavi Group Commander on 10 July 2026. Their appointment dates and reusable portraits remain unresolved. A separate operational Goa Group is not asserted without dated confirmation. [BMSCE NCC](https://bmsce.ac.in/home/BMSCE-NCC), [Goa government](https://dip.goa.gov.in/cm-address-ncc-meeting-held-recently/).

## Conflicts and unresolved records

- **1 Goa Remount and Veterinary Squadron:** recorded at Panaji under Belagavi in 2017. No reliable later corroboration was established. It remains a historical record, not an asserted current formation.
- **3 Karnataka Air Squadron (Technical), Bidar:** the official baseline establishes identity, parent and city in 2017. Later cadet submissions are leads but do not replace an authoritative current unit profile in this register. The record remains explicitly historical-only.
- **5 Karnataka Battalion:** the Ministry of Defence’s 2017–18 annual report lists Chikkaballapur in the accretion programme, while the SJCIT institutional page labels the battalion Bengaluru. The explorer preserves its supported Bengaluru B association but leaves its HQ unmapped pending resolution. - **15 Karnataka Battalion:** the AIT institutional affiliation document explicitly identifies Hassan and Mysuru Group; Christ University documents a July 2025 camp hosted by 15 Battalion at Hassan. A shorthand reference to “15 Kar Bn NCC, Mysuru” in a different camp report may describe group affiliation, so it is not used to relocate the HQ. [AIT](https://aitckm.edu.in/NAAC/aqar/Criterion3/3.4.4/3.4.3_and_3.4.4_CSE.pdf), [Christ University](https://brc.christuniversity.in/center/C/NCC/camp).
- **Goa naval and girls units:** the 2017 directory says Panaji; government directory pages used in the existing records say Mapusa. The current explorer retains Mapusa with source notes; this is not evidence of a known relocation date.

- **MY Bharat:** a Karnataka & Goa page presenting Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Warangal groups is rejected for hierarchy construction.
- **Portraits:** a NITK page identifies Lt Col Nikhil Khullar with a photograph, but its reuse rights were not established. The name is an explicitly undated profile. The explorer does not substitute an ANO’s portrait for an Officer Commanding.

## Geography and display

Directorate regions are formed from the Survey of India ABDB 2025 state/UT geometry and the separately sourced directorate remit. Geometry is generalized by 250 metres for overview use. Four source-designated interstate disputed polygons remain neutral. Headquarters are separately sourced city points; interior text anchors are cartographic layout positions, never headquarters. Group territorial boundaries are not fabricated. [Survey of India ABDB](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/administrative-boundary-data-base-abdb-).

Regional text boxes are verified to lie within the largest polygon of their directorate in the map projection. Disjoint islands retain their actual region geometry, while abbreviated mainland labels expand to the full name in the dossier. Very small regions become labeled only when the map has enough space; the complete directorate hierarchy and search remain available at every zoom.
