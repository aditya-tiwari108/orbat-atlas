# NCC: 19 approved directorates

Reviewed 19 September 2026.

The Ministry of Defence [announced approval on 15 June 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2272881) for independent Andhra Pradesh and Jharkhand directorates, increasing the total from 17 to 19. Both are included in the directorate index, search and regional map.

The map separates Andhra Pradesh from Telangana and Jharkhand from Bihar using Survey of India state geometry. These are **approved administrative remits**, not evidence that the new headquarters have opened. Both new records have `status: newly-approved`, no headquarters point, and no invented commander or portrait. Their regions can be selected and fitted to the viewport without an HQ coordinate.

The existing Hyderabad/Secunderabad and Patna office records retain their documented combined names and dated leadership evidence during this transition. Regional labels show the approved residual remits, Telangana and Bihar. The [MY Bharat NCC office directory](https://mybharat.gov.in/ncc) and [Bihar & Jharkhand Directorate](https://nccdtebj.in/) still showed combined organizations during this review; they do not independently establish the new offices' opening dates. Existing shared links remain valid.

The remaining 15 directorates are unchanged. All 36 states/UTs belong to exactly one approved remit. Four source-designated interstate disputed polygons remain neutral. Groups and units are organizational children; no group or battalion territory is inferred.

## Outstanding evidence

- Opening orders, headquarters addresses and leadership appointments for the two new directorates.
- Dated transfer orders establishing the new parent of individual groups and units.
- Updated predecessor-office titles and leadership after operational separation.

Karnataka & Goa remains one combined directorate. Its six groups and 55 unit records, including 4 Karnataka Engineer Company, are documented in the [separate field audit](ncc-karnataka-goa-audit.md).
