# Navy and Air Force expansion — field audit

Research checked 15 September 2026. This is a curated public-reference snapshot, not a certified current or complete order of battle. Check dates are not assertion dates. Individual records retain field evidence, source publication dates where available and unresolved gaps.

## Coverage

| Added in this release | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Flying squadrons | 40 |
| Air Force stations | 23 |
| Ships | 21 added; 27 total |
| Naval shore establishments | 19 added; 22 total including existing training establishments |
| Naval training squadron | 1 |

The dataset now contains 317 organizations. All seven Air Force commands and all four Navy-mode commands remain available. Maintenance Command is retained, but its repair depots are not expanded in this release. Numbers measure records, not organizational completeness.

## What the relationships mean

- An air station is mapped at a named city/locality center. No runway, shelter, aircraft position or facility footprint is represented.
- Squadron → station edges mean **based at**, not direct command. A separately cited `aviation.commandId` records the reported command affiliation. Intermediate wings are not invented.
- Naval shore establishment → command edges are command-level administrative grouping. Intermediate naval areas, NOICs and departments are not exhaustively modeled.
- Ship → fleet edges are sourced organizational associations. Ships have no coordinates. Commissioning location and exercise participation alone are not grounds for a fleet assignment.
- Training squadron ships follow the explicitly named January 2026 official release. No Coast Guard unit is silently reassigned to the Navy.

## Corrections and unresolved conflicts

- **INS Rajali**: Eastern Naval Command, supported by PIB, despite a conflicting position in the aggregate Wikipedia base list.
- **No. 18 Squadron**: Naliya, following its individual article and recent reporting. Older Sulur references are not used as its current base; the transfer date remains unresolved.
- **No. 4 Squadron**: aggregate list says Uttarlai but the individual article lists Jodhpur for Su-30MKI. Omitted pending resolution.
- **No. 223 Squadron**: conflicting basing references across the station and squadron lists. Omitted pending a focused update.
- **No. 41 Squadron**: its individual article names both Palam and Gauhati. Omitted pending resolution.
- **No. 59 Squadron**: aircraft conflict between aggregate list (Dornier) and individual article (HS-748). Omitted pending resolution.
- No. 33 and No. 78 squadron nicknames differ across reference pages. Their individual articles are used; nickname conflicts remain in record gaps.
- The aggregate Air Force **station** table explicitly identifies old 2009 source material; its embedded squadron assignments were not imported. Individual squadron articles supply base and aircraft fields; the separate active squadron list supplies reported command affiliations unless a stronger cited source exists.
- The Eastern Fleet ship list includes planned assignments and submarine associations that cannot safely be read as confirmed current fleet membership. It was not bulk-imported. In particular, Vikrant was not moved east based on a planned homeport.
- INS Nilgiri fleet assignment differs across references; no new record was introduced for it in this release.
- Helicopter units, UAV units, air-defence units, intermediate IAF wings, naval aviation squadrons, submarines and many smaller vessels remain outside this expansion.

## Editing and attribution

`data/india-naval-air.json` contains the expansion and its source registry; existing ship IDs remain in `data/india.json`. They are merged by `data/india.ts`. Facts have been condensed into original summaries; Wikipedia contributors are attributed through per-record article links and source records. Wikipedia text is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). No Wikipedia images were added in this release.

Source review is not proof of present-day assignments. The test suite checks structural consistency, source IDs, same-country base links, independent aircraft/command evidence, ship non-location, national map density and navigation. It cannot establish factual accuracy.
