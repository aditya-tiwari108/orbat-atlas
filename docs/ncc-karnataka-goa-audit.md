# Karnataka & Goa NCC audit — 8 September 2026

This is one combined directorate. Its six groups are Bengaluru A, Bengaluru B, Belagavi, Ballari, Mangaluru and Mysuru. The group list is corroborated by BMSCE’s institutional NCC page. The Goa government’s 10 July 2026 release identifies Air Commodore Kamal Chadha as DDG and Colonel C. Rajashekar as Belagavi Group Commander; neither appointment date is established.

## Applied records

Six groups and nineteen units have been added from institutional and government publications. Sources are attached separately to hierarchy relationships and headquarters cities. Bengaluru A and B deliberately share a city-level anchor; no arbitrary coordinate offsets distinguish their offices.

Verified unit relationships in this pass: Bengaluru A — 1 Karnataka Signal Regiment and 2 Karnataka Battalion; Bengaluru B — 5, 7 and 8 Karnataka Battalions; Mangaluru — 18 Karnataka Battalion and 6 Karnataka Naval Unit; Belagavi — 1 Goa Battalion, 1 Goa Girls Battalion and 1 Goa Naval Unit. The naval unit’s Belagavi association is recorded in the 2017 parliamentary directory and a 2021 cadet submission on MyGov; its current parent remains explicitly unverified. Four unit headquarters remain unplaced pending corroboration.

Further additions: Bengaluru B — 39 Karnataka Battalion (REVA and BMSIT); Ballari — 32 Karnataka Battalion in Kalaburagi (BVB institutional review, 2023) and 34 Karnataka Battalion in Ballari (BITM report, parent dated to the parliamentary directory); Mysuru — 13 and 14 Karnataka Battalions, 3 Karnataka Girls Battalion, 3 Karnataka Naval Unit, 4 Karnataka Air Squadron and 1 Karnataka Artillery Battery (January 2025 Mysuru Group reporting, with 13 Battalion corroborated by DPS in March 2026). Mysuru unit HQ cities are explicitly dated to the 2017 parliamentary directory and require a current recheck.

The official [Lok Sabha answer of 28 December 2017, Q1791](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/13/AU1791.pdf?source=pqals) provides a systematic historical baseline. It is not imported wholesale as a 2026 roster.

This is partial unit coverage. Empty groups are not evidence that those groups have no units. College NCC contingents are not counted as independent battalions.

## Conflicting or insufficient evidence

- MY Bharat’s Karnataka & Goa page incorrectly presents Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Warangal groups. Rejected as a source for the group hierarchy.
- BMSCE’s officer names include an older DDG and conflict with the dated 2026 Goa government announcement. Its organizational list is used; its older leadership is not promoted to current incumbency.
- SJCIT places 5 Karnataka Battalion in Bengaluru, while other material describes Chikkaballapur. Its group relationship is supported; its HQ remains unmapped.
- A third-party FlipHTML5 upload titled *Vijaytarang NCC Temp_compressed* supplies useful research leads but has obvious duplicated Mysuru entries and labels multiple Goa/Belagavi units as Dharwad in its map. The upload’s authority and publication status are unverified. It is not imported wholesale and is not a basis for new officer portraits.
- Goa iGOOD officer profiles conflict with the names in that upload. No new commander is selected merely because a site-wide “last modified” date is recent.
- Group territorial boundaries have not been established. State coverage must not be partitioned using HQ locations or unit distribution.
