This is a public DGNCC document hosted on a third-party mirror; the provenance is explicit. [June 2026 approval](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2272881) establishes the announced AP and Jharkhand directorates, not their operational headquarters.
